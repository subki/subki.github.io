Pocket Ledger Privacy Policy

Developer: Subki (Tangerang, Indonesia) · Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Pocket Ledger ("the app") handles information. The app is made by Subki, an independent developer. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information stored on your device

The app works offline. It stores the expenses, income, accounts, budgets and settings you enter only on your device, in the app's local storage. We do not have a server for this app, we do not create accounts, and we never receive or see this information. Uninstalling the app or using its "reset data" option deletes it. If you choose to export or share something from the app, it is sent only to the app or person you pick in Android's share menu.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version) and diagnostics,
  • interactions with ads (views and taps).

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings screen ("Privacy options").

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

Data stored by the app stays on your device until you delete it. Data processed by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's retention policies and is transmitted using encryption in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
